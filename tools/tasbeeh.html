About the Tasbeeh Counter

A simple digital tasbeeh for your dhikr. Choose a preset or set your own target, count with a tap or the keyboard, and follow your daily and total counts. سبحة إلكترونية مجانية بدون تسجيل.

Features

  • Presets: سبحان الله، الحمد لله، الله أكبر (33), أستغفر الله، لا إله إلا الله (100)
  • Custom target for any count
  • Today, total and completed counters kept on your device
  • Optional vibration feedback on phones
  • Count with a tap, or Space and Enter on a keyboard

How to use it

  1. Pick a dhikr preset or set your own target.
  2. Tap Count, or press Space or Enter.
  3. Watch your progress; reset any time.
